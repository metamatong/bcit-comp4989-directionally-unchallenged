# Project walkthrough

1. Take or upload a photo containing a nearby room sign. Once prepared locally, the app advances straight to the destination screen.
2. Enter a destination such as `SW03 2605`. Stairs are preferred by default; toggle **Use elevators** to require an elevator route. If no elevator route is mapped, the app reports that instead of using stairs.
3. Tap **Get Directions**. The browser requests GPS automatically. The configured provider reads the photo once for labels and visible features. GPS narrows the building, and the app selects the strongest source match without asking the visitor to pick a start.
4. A complete walking route opens on the first guided step with an edge-to-edge floor map and thumb-zone bottom sheet, and starts live tracking. The current step selects the floor map. During Go, select **3D view → Preview full route** to play the journey from the start, including both ends of each transfer. The angled maps use vector linework and lettering exported from the original pilot PDFs so room labels stay sharp when enlarged. Direction arrows sit inside the blue walking line, and raised wall faces hide the route where they overlap it. Back to guidance returns to your walking step. A fresh GPS reading can show a dot on a geographically aligned floor. Use **Previous**, **Next step**, and **Finish route** to control progress; tapping **Done** on the finished screen returns immediately to the photo start screen. **Overview** opens the full vertical timeline itinerary and floor maps in travel order, and **Back to steps** returns to your current instruction.
5. Cross-building travel uses only the mapped building connections. A missing photo match, destination approach or route connection is explained in the app.
6. **Start over**, destination editing, and location evidence live neatly inside **Overview** (or below fallback messages) to keep active step-by-step walking uncluttered.

The catalogue covers all 89 supplied Burnaby plans, with approximate GPS alignments available on 65. The walkthrough uses SE12, SW03, SW05 and SW01, which all have building footprints. See [device-location coverage](device-location.md).
